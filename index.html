Welcome to BINGO

Bingo rules:

Players can win by covering a complete horizontal, vertical or diagonal line. Pattern bingo is often played in 75-ball games.

To start, select the amount you are willing to bet from the top. The first square will be called and this is where you start verifiying if you have the square announced. While the other players automatically fill in the squares their card has, you need to locate the square called on your card and click it to move on. If you do not have this square, select any of your squares or "Select Next Square" to move on.

Airplane mode
  • Start the game in a browser window. Do not use incognito mode.
  • Once game has loaded, you can switch your device to airplane mode.
  • The game runs entirely in your browser and will continue to function normally after this point.
